# Celar AI Identity and Origin

**Status:** Documentation-first rebrand baseline  
**Classification:** US Signal Internal — Confidential

## Core identity

Celar AI is the unified operational intelligence system for the US Signal Solution Provider division. Conceived and engineered under the direction of **Dr. Ahmed Adeyemi, Manager of Professional Services**, the system was built to serve as the central intersection where consulting teams convene, collaborate, and exchange critical project information.

## Meaning behind the name

The name draws from **Celeritas**, the Latin concept of swiftness or speed, and from the conventional symbol **c** for the speed of light in `E=mc²`.

- **US Signal connection:** The identity pays homage to US Signal's fiber-network and digital-infrastructure foundation.
- **Professional Services mission:** Celar AI translates the speed of light into the **speed of delivery**, reducing the elapsed time required to scope, plan, execute, troubleshoot, report, and invoice customer solutions.

## Catalyst: overcoming the Changepoint struggle

Changepoint served as a functional legacy professional-services automation platform and system of record, but it also created operational drag:

- **Siloed information:** Teams often worked across disconnected sources and manually transferred context.
- **Administration tax:** Consultants spent technical time navigating rigid menus to enter time, maintain tasks, or understand project financial health.
- **Speed bottleneck:** SOW drafting, project handoff, task assignment, time administration, billing readiness, and invoice routing depended on slower manual workflows.

Celar AI addresses this struggle by unifying authorized documents, live system data, workflows, troubleshooting evidence, reporting, financial context, and AI-assisted reasoning inside one governed operating layer.
